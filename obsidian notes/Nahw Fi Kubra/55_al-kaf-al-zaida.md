# 55 — الكافُ الزائدةُ

> **Video:** [الكاف الزائدة](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hL4VdZINTFs)
> **Book:** الحرف الزائد — الرابع: الكاف (p.58)
> **Topic:** The fourth and last redundant particle — the **كاف**, which still carries an emphasis of **likeness**.

---

> **الرابعُ: الكافُ الزائدةُ** — نحو: ﴿ليسَ **كـ**مِثلِه شيءٌ﴾.

- In ﴿laysa **ka**-mithlihi shayʾ﴾, the كاف is **redundant**: the meaning is *"there is nothing like Him."* (If the كاف were meaningful, "like the like of Him" would imply He *has* a like — so it is taken as **زائدة for emphasis**.)
- *(Note: the redundant kāf still reinforces the sense of **تشبيه** — see the meaningful [[47_al-kaf|kāf]].)*
