# 18 — الفعلُ اللازم (The Intransitive Verb)

> **Video:** [الفعل اللازم](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdREJwvvrbw)
> **Book:** المقصد الأول، الباب الأول في الفعل (p.22)
> **Topic:** Opens **al-Maqṣad al-Awwal** (the operators in detail). The verb is **قياسي** (rule-governed); the قياسي splits into **lāzim** (intransitive) and متعدٍّ (transitive). This lesson covers the lāzim.

---

## Context: the قياسي operator
- The **قياسي** verb is one with a **consistent rule (قاعدة مطردة)**: it **raises** the فاعل (and its likes) and **sets in naṣب** the objects and the ḥāl.
- It divides into **اللازم** (intransitive) and **[[19_al-fil-al-mutaaddi|المتعدي]]** (transitive).
> *(The Maqṣad opens by recapping the 5 mabādiʾ from [[15_al-amil-wa-ghayr-al-amil|العامل]]: all verbs operate; some particles & some nouns operate; sentences & quasi-sentences never operate.)*

## الفعل اللازم

> **اللازم: ما لا يتجاوزُ أثرُ فاعلِه إلى المفعولِ به** — its effect does **not pass beyond the doer** to an object.
- Also called **فعل قاصر** (deficient) and **فعل غير متعدٍّ** (non-transitive).
- Its effect *can* reach **non-objects** — a ظرف or a حال: *جلسَ الطالبُ **أمامَ المعلمِ متأدبًا***.

> *The qiyāsī is that which has a **consistent rule**.*

![[n18-l6.png]]
> *And it is of two kinds:*

![[n18-l7.png]]
> *The first kind: the **intransitive verb** (al-fiʿl al-lāzim).*

![[n18-l8.png]]
> *It is that whose effect does **not pass beyond its doer** to an object — e.g. ﴿wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn﴾ (nor will they grieve).*

![[n18-l9.png]]
> *The intransitive verb is [also] called a **deficient verb** (fiʿl qāṣir) and a **non-transitive verb** (fiʿl ghayr mutaʿaddٍ).*

![[n18-l10.png]]
> *The effect of an intransitive verb's doer may pass to something **other than** an object — like the ẓarf or the ḥāl — e.g. "jalasa ṭ-ṭālibu amāma l-muʿallimi mutaʾaddiban tabjīlan li-l-ʿilm" (the student sat before the teacher, respectfully, revering knowledge).*
