# 11 — الجملةُ الكُبْرى والصُّغْرى (Major & Minor Sentences)

> **Video:** [الجملة الكبرى والصغرى](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YI8vaMgdzVc)
> **Book:** المسألة الثالثة: أقسام الجملة باعتبار نسبتها إلى جملة أخرى (p.13)
> **Topic:** The third division — by a sentence's **relation to another sentence**: does it *contain* a sentence, or *sit inside* one?

---

## The division (by relation to another sentence)

- **الجملة الكُبْرى (major):** a sentence that **contains another sentence** within it.
- **الجملة الصُّغْرى (minor):** a sentence that **occupies the place of a single word (مفرد)** inside the major one.

**Worked example:** ﴿واللهُ **يَعْلَمُ**﴾
- The whole — *Allāhu yaʿlamu* — is the **كبرى** (the مبتدأ *Allāh* + a predicate that is itself a sentence).
- The inner verbal sentence **يَعْلَمُ** (he knows) is the **صغرى** — it fills the خبر slot, i.e. the place of a single word.

> So *kubrā* and *ṣughrā* are **relative** labels: the same clause can be ṣughrā relative to what contains it and kubrā relative to what it contains.

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.13

![[n11-l1.png]]
> *Issue Three: the kinds of sentence with respect to its **relation to another sentence**.*

![[n11-l2.png]]
> *It is of two kinds:*

![[n11-l3.png]]
> *The first kind: the **major sentence** (al-jumlah al-kubrā).*

![[n11-l4.png]]
> *It is that which **contains a [whole] sentence** — e.g. ﴿wa-llāhu yaʿlam﴾ (And Allah knows).*

![[n11-l5.png]]
> *The second kind: the **minor sentence** (al-jumlah al-ṣughrā).*

![[n11-l6.png]]
> *It is that which **falls in the place of a single word** within the major one — e.g. ﴿wa-llāhu **yaʿlam**﴾ (the clause "yaʿlam" filling the predicate slot).*
