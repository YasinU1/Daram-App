# 06 — الفِعْلُ وعَلاماتُه (The Verb and its Signs)

> **Video:** [الفعل وعلاماته](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6Lw-Dt6r9g)
> **Book:** القسم الثاني: الفعل (p.8–9)
> **Topic:** The second class of word — its definition (a meaning **tied to time**) and the **signs** that identify a verb, sorted by tense.

---

## Definition

> **الفِعْلُ: ما دَلَّ على معنًى في نفسِه مُقترنٍ بزمانٍ.**

A word denoting a meaning **coupled with a time** — the mirror image of the [[03_al-ism-wa-alamatuhu|اسم]] (which is *time-independent*).

**The verb contains three aspects** (book fāʾidah, p.8; stressed in the video):
1. **الحَدَث** — the *event/action* itself (this is the meaning of the **مصدر**), e.g. the act of worship in **عَبَدَ**.
2. **الزَّمَن** — the *time* (past / present / …) carried by its form.
3. **النِّسبة إلى الفاعل** — it is always *linked to a doer* (you never have a verb without an implied فاعل).

> Example set: **عَبَدَ** (past) · **يَعْبُدُ** (present) · **اعْبُدْ** (command).

---

## The signs of a verb (علامات الفعل), by tense

| Category | Signs |
|---|---|
| **General (العامّة)** | conjugates into ماضٍ/مضارع/أمر/نهي · accepts overt nominative pronouns (آمَنْتُ، آمِنوا) |
| **Past only (الماضي)** | the quiescent **تاء التأنيث** (آمَنَتْ) |
| **Present only (المضارع)** | **لَمْ، لَنْ، السين، سوف** · begins with a **مضارعة** letter (نأتي = ن/أ/ت/ي) · the two **نونا التوكيد** |
| **Past & present** | **قَدْ** |
| **Command only (الأمر)** | denotes a demand **and** accepts **ياء المخاطَبة** (اُدْخُلي) |

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.8–9 (الفعل وعلاماته)

The matn line by line, with translation:

![[n06-l1.png]]
> *The second kind [of word]: the verb (al-fiʿl).*

![[n06-l2.png]]
> *It is that which denotes a meaning in itself coupled with a time — e.g. **ʿabada** (he worshipped), **yaʿbudu** (he worships), **uʿbud** (worship!).*

![[n06-l3.png]]
> *Among the **general** signs of the verb:*

![[n06-l4.png]]
> *its conjugation into past, present, command, and prohibition;*

![[n06-l5.png]]
> *and the attachment of overt nominative pronouns — e.g. **āmantu** (I believed), **āminū** (believe, you all!).*

![[n06-l6.png]]
> *Among its signs **specific to the past tense**:*

![[n06-l7.png]]
> *the attachment of the quiescent tāʾ of femininity — e.g. ﴿**āmanat**﴾ (she believed).*

![[n06-l8.png]]
> *Among its signs **specific to the present tense**:*

![[n06-l9.png]]
> ***lam** — e.g. ﴿lam yalid wa-lam yūlad﴾ (He neither begets nor is born).*

![[n06-l10.png]]
> *and **lan** — e.g. ﴿lan yuṣībanā illā mā kataba-llāhu lanā﴾ (Nothing will befall us except what Allah has decreed for us).*

![[n06-l11.png]]
> *and the prefix **sa-** — e.g. ﴿sa-yarḥamuhumu-llāh﴾ (Allah will have mercy on them).*

![[n06-l12.png]]
> *and **sawfa** — e.g. ﴿sawfa astaghfiru lakum rabbī﴾ (I shall ask forgiveness for you from my Lord).*

![[n06-l13.png]]
> *and its beginning with a present-tense prefix letter — gathered in "**na-ʾtī**" (ن/أ/ت/ي) — e.g. ﴿wa-llāhu **yu**ḥyī wa-**yu**mīt﴾ (Allah gives life and causes death).*

![[n06-l14.png]]
> *and the two **nūns of emphasis** — e.g. ﴿la-yusjana**nna** wa-la-yakūna**n** mina ṣ-ṣāghirīn﴾ (he shall surely be imprisoned and be among the abased).*

![[n06-l15.png]]
> *Among its signs **specific to both past and present**:*

![[n06-l16.png]]
> ***qad** — e.g. ﴿la-qad samiʿa-llāh﴾ (Allah has surely heard) and ﴿qad yaʿlamu-llāh﴾ (Allah does know).*

![[n06-l17.png]]
> *Among its signs **specific to the command**:*

![[n06-l18.png]]
> *its denoting a demand while accepting the **yāʾ of the (feminine) addressee** — e.g. ﴿wa-**dkhulī** jannatī﴾ (and enter, [O feminine addressee], My Paradise).*
