# 13 — الظرفُ اللغوُ والمُستقَرُّ فيه (Laghw & Mustaqarr Adverbials)

> **Video:** [الظرف اللغو والظرف المستقر فيه](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmkFnKG2mws)
> **Book:** المسألة الثانية: أقسام شبه الجملة باعتبار عاملها (p.14–15)
> **Topic:** The second division of the **شبه الجملة** — by its **operator (عامل)**: is the operator a real verb/word elsewhere (laghw), or a deleted "exists/is settled" (mustaqarr)?

---

## Terminology
- The quasi-sentence's **operator** is the **مُتعلَّق**; the quasi-sentence itself (as it sits in the clause) is the **مُتعلِّق**.

## The two kinds

### الظرف اللغو (non-predicative)
Its operator is a **real verb/word** — either **stated** (مذكور) or a **specific deleted** one (محذوف مخصوص). It carries **no hidden pronoun** referring back to a subject (that's why it's "laghw").
- Stated: ﴿لقد **أرسَلْنا** نوحًا إلى قومه﴾.
- Specific deleted: ﴿وإلى عادٍ أخاهم هودًا﴾ → i.e. **أرسَلْنا**.

> **Where the laghw operator MUST be deleted** (book, p.14):
> | # | Case | Example | Estimated |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | الأمثال (proverbs) | الكلابُ على البقرِ | أي سُلِّط |
> | 2 | العبارات المأثورة | بأبي أنتَ وأمي | أي مَفدِيٌّ |
> | 3 | القسم بغير الباء | ﴿تاللهِ تَفتَأُ تَذكُرُ يوسفَ﴾ | (oath operator dropped) |
> | 4 | خبر كان مع لام الجحود | ﴿ما كانَ اللهُ لِيُعَذِّبَهم وأنتَ فيهم﴾ | أي قاصدًا |

### الظرف المستقر فيه (predicative)
Its operator is a **general deleted** one meaning *to exist / be settled* — and it **carries a hidden pronoun**.
- ﴿إنَّكَ على صراطٍ مستقيمٍ﴾ → i.e. **تَثبُتُ / ثابتٌ**.
- The deleted general operator = a **verb** (استقرَّ، ثَبَتَ، وُجِدَ) or a **noun acting as verb** (مستقِرٌّ، ثابتٌ، موجودٌ، كائنٌ) — **except in a relative clause (صلة)**, where it must be a **verb** only (الذي في مكةَ = استقرَّ, not مستقِرٌّ).

> **The 5 places a mustaqarr adverbial occurs** (p.15): الخبر (& what's like it) · الحال · النعت · الصلة · الاعتماد (after a hamza of question, etc., e.g. ﴿أفي اللهِ شَكٌّ﴾).

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.14–15 (core matn)

![[n13-l1.png]]
> *Issue Two: the kinds of quasi-sentence with respect to its **operator**.*

![[n13-l2.png]]
> *The operator of the quasi-sentence is called the **mutaʿallaq**; the quasi-sentence occurring in the clause is called the **mutaʿalliq**.*

![[n13-l3.png]]
> *It is of two kinds:*

![[n13-l4.png]]
> *The first kind: the **non-predicative adverbial** (al-ẓarf al-laghw).*

![[n13-l5.png]]
> *It is a quasi-sentence whose operator is:*

![[n13-l6.png]]
> ***stated** — e.g. ﴿la-qad **arsalnā** Nūḥan ilā qawmih﴾ (We did send Noah to his people);*

![[n13-l7.png]]
> *or **a specific deleted [operator]** — e.g. ﴿wa-ilā ʿĀdin akhāhum Hūdā﴾ → i.e. **arsalnā** (we sent).*

![[n13-l8.png]]
> *The second kind: the **predicative adverbial** (al-ẓarf al-mustaqarr fīh).*

![[n13-l9.png]]
> *It is a quasi-sentence whose operator is a **general deleted** one — e.g. ﴿innaka ʿalā ṣirāṭin mustaqīm﴾ → i.e. **tathbutu / thābit** (you are firmly established).*

![[n13-l10.png]]
> *[Note] the word "mustaqarr" [denotes] the deleted operator, while a "ẓarf mustaqarr" is a quasi-sentence whose operator is estimated.*
