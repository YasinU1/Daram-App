# 30 — ما بَرِحَ وما انفكَّ وما زالَ وما فَتِئَ (Verbs of Continuity)

> **Video:** [ما برح وما انفك ومازال ومافتئ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yzDvJyytf8)
> **Book:** كان وأخواتها — الثامن–الحادي عشر (p.32)
> **Topic:** Four sisters of [[27_kana|كان]] that denote **continuity (الاستمرار)** — "X *did not cease* to be Y."

---

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.32

![[n30-l1.png]]
> *The eighth: **mā bariḥa** — for **continuity** — e.g. ﴿qālū lan **nabraḥa** ʿalayhi ʿākifīn﴾ (they said: "we will not cease being devoted to it").*

![[n30-l2.png]]
> *The ninth: **mā-nfakka** — for continuity — e.g. mā-nfakka l-mudhnibu rājiyan (the sinner did not cease hoping).*

![[n30-l3.png]]
> *The tenth: **mā zāla** — for continuity — e.g. ﴿fa-mā **ziltum** fī shakk﴾ (you have not ceased to be in doubt).*

![[n30-l4.png]]
> *The eleventh: **mā fatiʾa** — for continuity — e.g. ﴿ta-llāhi **taftaʾu** tadhkuru Yūsuf﴾ (By Allah, you will not cease remembering Joseph).*

![[n30-l5.png]]
> *These four:*

![[n30-l6.png]]
> *are **defective in conjugation** — only the past and present come from them (no command, etc.).*

## مسألة — when do they operate?
> They **only act** as deficient verbs (نواسخ) when **preceded by a نفي (negation), نهي (prohibition), or دعاء (supplication)**.
- This is why they normally appear as **ما** بَرِح / **ما** زال … — the **ما** is the negation. (Without it, *zāla* etc. is a complete verb meaning "to depart/cease".)
