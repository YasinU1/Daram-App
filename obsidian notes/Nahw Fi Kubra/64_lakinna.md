# 64 — لكنَّ (Lākinna)

> **Video:** [لكن](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BjujUVLLiQ)
> **Book:** الرابع: لكنَّ (p.65)
> **Topic:** The fourth sister — the particle of **rectification / amendment (الاستدراك)**.

---

> **الرابعُ: لكنَّ، وهي حرفٌ للاستدراكِ.**

**معنى الاستدراك:** following a first statement with one that **removes a false impression** — of its affirmation or its negation — that the first might suggest:
- «ما زيدٌ قويًّا **لكنّه** شجاعٌ» — *"Zayd is not strong"* might suggest cowardice, so: *"…**but** he is brave."*

> **Usually preceded by الواو** in the Qurʾān: ﴿وما كفرَ سليمانُ **ولكنَّ** الشياطينَ كفروا﴾.

Like its sisters, it takes an **اسم منصوب** + **خبر مرفوع**.
