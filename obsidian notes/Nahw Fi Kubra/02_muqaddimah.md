# 02 — مُقَدِّمَةُ الكِتاب (Introduction): اللفظ، القول، الكلمة

> **Video:** [مقدمة الكتاب](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gj7Ko3hORDs)
> **Book:** الباب الأول في الكلمة (p.6)
> **Topic:** The foundational vocabulary of grammar — *utterance*, *meaningful word*, *speech* — and how words divide into **مُفْرَد** (single) vs **مُرَكَّب** (compound), ending with the three kinds of word.

---

## The ladder of terms (from broadest to narrowest)

Each term narrows the one before it:

### 1. اللَّفْظ (al-lafẓ) — *utterance / sound*
> **اللَّفْظُ: كلُّ ما خَرَجَ مِن فمِ الإنسانِ مِن صوتٍ.**

Anything that comes out of the mouth as sound — **whether or not it means anything** (snoring, a baby's babbling — all of it is **لَفْظ**).

اللفظ splits into two:

| Type | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Meaningless | **المُهْمَل** (al-muhmal) | a sound with no assigned meaning — e.g. a baby's babble |
| Meaningful | **المَوْضُوع** (al-mawḍūʿ) | a sound **coined for a meaning** (وُضِعَ لِمَعْنًى) |

### 2. القَوْل (al-qawl) — *meaningful utterance*
> **القَوْلُ: لفظٌ دالٌّ على مَعنًى.**

- **زيد** denotes a person, **قَلَم** a pen, **وَرَق** paper. Each is a قَوْل.
- A baby's babble is **not** a قول (no meaning).

---

## القَوْل divides by structure: مُفْرَد vs مُرَكَّب

### المُفْرَد (al-mufrad) — *single / simple*
> **المُفْرَدُ: ما لا تَدُلُّ أجزاؤُه على مَعنًى، نحو: رَجُلٌ — فلا تَدُلُّ الراءُ أو الجيمُ أو اللامُ على معنًى.**

A word whose **individual parts (letters) do NOT each denote part of the meaning**. In **رَجُل**, the ر / ج / ل carry no separate meanings; only the whole denotes the man.

### المُرَكَّب (al-murakkab) — *compound*
> **المُرَكَّبُ: ما تَدُلُّ أجزاؤُه على مَعنًى، نحو: كِتابُ الوَلَدِ — فإنَّ "كِتاب" يَدُلُّ على معنًى و"الوَلَد" يَدُلُّ على معنًى.**

A construction whose **parts each denote a separate meaning** (e.g. **عَبْدُ اللَّهِ** — *slave* + *God*).

> ⚠️ **Two different senses of "مُفْرَد" — don't confuse them:**
> 1. **In number (العَدَد):** مُفْرَد = **singular** (وَاحِد), opposite of **المُثَنّى** (dual) and **الجَمْع** (plural).
> 2. **In structure (التركيب):** مُفْرَد = a **single word**, opposite of **المُرَكَّب**.
>
> *(Book fāʾidah, p.6: a مُرَكَّب can itself be مُفْرَد in another sense — e.g. **مزج** compounds like بَعْلَبَك; and number-compounds split into مزج (أحد عشر…تسعة عشر) and عطف (أحد وعشرون…).)*

---

## القَوْل / الكَلِمة divides by kind: the three classes of word

> **وهي ثلاثةُ أقسامٍ:**

| # | Arabic | Translit. | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **الاسْم** | al-ism | noun |
| 2 | **الفِعْل** | al-fiʿl | verb |
| 3 | **الحَرْف** | al-ḥarf | particle |

> **الكَلِمةُ ثَلاثةُ أقسامٍ: اسمٌ وفِعلٌ وحَرفٌ.** — Each gets its own treatment next, starting with **[[03_al-ism-wa-alamatuhu|الاسم]]**.

---

## Quick map

```
اللفظ (any utterance)
├── مُهْمَل  (meaningless)
└── مَوْضوع → القَوْل (meaningful)
        ├── by structure:  مُفْرَد  ↔  مُرَكَّب
        └── by kind (الكلمة):  اسم — فِعل — حَرف
```

> *And it [the word] is of three kinds:*

![[n02-l9.png]]
> *The first kind: the noun (al-ism).*

![[n02-l10.png]]
> *It is that which denotes a meaning in itself, not coupled with a time.*
