# 23 — ألم تَرَ وأَرَأَيْتَ (Special Heart-Verb Forms)

> **Video:** [ألم تر وأرأيت](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NwoQJzmnLho)
> **Book:** p.26
> **Topic:** Three frozen Qurʾānic expressions built on **رأى/علم** — their special meanings and pronoun behaviour.

---

| Form | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **ألم تَرَ** | "do you not know?" | conveys **amazement**; "knowing" expressed via *seeing* (sight = strongest cause of knowledge) |
| **أرأيتَ** | "tell me!" (أخبرني) | used in **wondrous** situations; its **nominative pronoun changes** (أرأيتَ، أرأيتِ…) |
| **أرأيتَك** | "tell me!" + emphasis | the **kāf of address** is added; the **pronoun does *not* change** — only the kāf does |

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.26

![[n23-l1.png]]
> ***alam tara** means "do you not know?" and carries a sense of **amazement**; knowing is expressed through **seeing** (ar-ruʾyah) because sight is the strongest cause of knowledge.*

![[n23-l2.png]]
> ***a-raʾayta** comes meaning "**tell me!**" (akhbirnī) in wondrous situations, and its **nominative pronoun changes** — ﴿a-raʾayta mani-ttakhadha ilāhahu hawāh﴾ (have you seen the one who takes his desire as his god?).*

![[n23-l3.png]]
> ***a-raʾaytaka** — *a-raʾayta* with the **kāf of address** for emphasis; here the **pronoun does not change**, only the kāf does (a-raʾaytaka, a-raʾaytakum).*
