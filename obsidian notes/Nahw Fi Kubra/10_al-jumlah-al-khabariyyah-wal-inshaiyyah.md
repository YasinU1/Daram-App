# 10 — الجملةُ الخبريةُ والإنشائية (Declarative & Performative Sentences)

> **Video:** [الجملة الخبرية والإنشائية](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yfMUax1Bu9k)
> **Book:** المسألة الثانية: أقسام الجملة باعتبار معناها (p.12)
> **Topic:** The second division of the sentence — by its **meaning**: can it be judged true/false, or not?

---

## The division (by meaning / معنى)

| | الجملة الخبرية (declarative) | الجملة الإنشائية (performative) |
|---|---|---|
| Test | the speaker **can be** truthful **or** lying (truth-evaluable) | the speaker is **neither** truthful nor lying (not truth-evaluable) |
| Example | ﴿إياك نعبد وإياك نستعين﴾ | a question, command, wish, oath, … |

**The إنشائية types** (book list): الاستفهام (question) · التمني (wish) · النداء (call) · التحذير (warning) · الإغراء (incitement) · الأمر (command) · النهي (prohibition) · الدعاء (supplication) · صيغ المدح والذم (praise/blame) · صيغ الترجي والتفجع (hope/lament) · القسم (oath).

> **خبر/إنشاء — wording vs. meaning** (book masʾalah, p.12): a sentence can be **declarative in form but performative in intent** — ﴿الحمدُ لله رب العالمين﴾ (grammatically a statement, but *intends praise*); or **performative in form but declarative in intent** — «مَن كَذَبَ عليَّ مُتعمِّدًا فليَتبوَّأْ مقعدَه من النار» (grammatically a command, but *informs* of a ruling).

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.12

![[n10-l1.png]]
> *Issue Two: the kinds of sentence with respect to its **meaning**.*

![[n10-l2.png]]
> *It is of two kinds:*

![[n10-l3.png]]
> *The first kind: the **declarative** sentence (al-khabariyyah).*

![[n10-l4.png]]
> *It is one in which the speaker may be truthful or lying — e.g. ﴿iyyāka naʿbudu wa-iyyāka nastaʿīn﴾ (You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help).*

![[n10-l5.png]]
> *The second kind: the **performative** sentence (al-inshāʾiyyah).*

![[n10-l6.png]]
> *It is one in which the speaker is neither truthful nor lying; and it includes:*

![[n10-l7.png]]
> ***interrogation** — e.g. ﴿a-ilāhun maʿa-llāh﴾ (Is there a god with Allah?);*

![[n10-l8.png]]
> *and **wishing** — e.g. ﴿yā layta qawmī yaʿlamūn﴾ (Would that my people knew!);*

![[n10-l9.png]]
> *and **calling** — e.g. ﴿yā ayyuhā r-rasūl﴾ (O Messenger!);*

![[n10-l10.png]]
> *and **warning** — e.g. ﴿nāqata-llāhi wa-suqyāhā﴾ ([Beware] the she-camel of Allah and her drink!);*

![[n10-l11.png]]
> *and **incitement** — e.g. al-ʿilma al-ʿilm ([Pursue] knowledge, knowledge!);*

![[n10-l12.png]]
> *and **command** — e.g. ﴿wa-aqīmū ṣ-ṣalāh﴾ (And establish the prayer);*

![[n10-l13.png]]
> *and **prohibition** — e.g. ﴿wa-lā taqrabū l-fawāḥish﴾ (And do not approach indecencies);*

![[n10-l14.png]]
> *and **supplication** — e.g. ﴿salāmun ʿalaykum﴾ (Peace be upon you);*

![[n10-l15.png]]
> *and the **formulas of praise & blame** — e.g. ﴿wa-la-niʿma dāru l-muttaqīn﴾ (And excellent is the home of the God-conscious);*

![[n10-l16.png]]
> *and the **formulas of hope & lament** — e.g. ﴿yā ḥasratā ʿalā mā farraṭtu fī janbi-llāh﴾ (Oh, my regret for what I neglected toward Allah!);*

![[n10-l17.png]]
> *and the **oath** — e.g. ﴿wa-l-ʿaṣr﴾ (By time!).*
