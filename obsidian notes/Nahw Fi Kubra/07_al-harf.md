# 07 — الحَرْف (The Particle)

> **Video:** [الحرف](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h5W19InfrGU)
> **Book:** القسم الثالث: الحرف (p.9–10)
> **Topic:** The third class of word — defined by what it **lacks**, plus the senses in which the word *ḥarf* is used.

---

## Definition & sign

- **الحَرْف:** a word that denotes a meaning **in something else** (في غيره) — it has no independent meaning; its meaning appears only in combination. E.g. **في** (in), **إنَّ** (indeed).
- **Sign (by elimination):** a ḥarf is known by having **none** of the [[03_al-ism-wa-alamatuhu|signs of the noun]] or the [[06_al-fil-wa-alamatuhu|signs of the verb]] — e.g. **هل، بل**.

## The word *ḥarf* has several senses
1. **حروف المباني** — the *spelling letters* / alphabet (ا، ب، ت …).
2. **حروف المعاني** — the *meaning-particles* (في، إنَّ، بل، لا) — this is the ḥarf of grammar.
3. **حرف المدّ / حرف اللين** — letters of prolongation/softness (a phonetic sense).

---

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.9–10 (الحرف)

![[n07-l1.png]]
> *The third kind [of word]: the particle (al-ḥarf).*

![[n07-l2.png]]
> *It is that which denotes a meaning **in something else** — e.g. **fī** (in), **inna** (indeed).*

![[n07-l3.png]]
> *The particle is recognised by **none** of the signs of the noun or the verb being found in it — e.g. **hal** (?), **bal** (rather).*

![[n07-l4.png]]
> *The word "ḥarf" is applied to:*

![[n07-l5.png]]
> ***letters of spelling** (ḥurūf al-mabānī) — the alphabet letters — e.g. fāʾ, ʿayn, lām;*

![[n07-l6.png]]
> *and **particles of meaning** (ḥurūf al-maʿānī) — those that denote a meaning — e.g. fī, inna, bal, lā.*

![[n07-l7.png]]
> *A **letter of prolongation** (ḥarf madd) is a quiescent weak-letter (ا/و/ي) preceded by a matching vowel — e.g. ﴿wa-**kū**nū maʿa ṣ-ṣādiqīn﴾.*

![[n07-l8.png]]
> *A **letter of softness** (ḥarf līn) is a quiescent wāw or yāʾ preceded by a fatḥa — e.g. al-kha**y**r (the yāʾ of khayr).*
