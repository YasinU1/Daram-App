# 12 — شِبْهُ الجملة (The Quasi-Sentence)

> **Video:** [شبه الجملة](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dIc2JZCXpnI)
> **Book:** الفصل الثاني في شبه الجملة (p.13)
> **Topic:** The "quasi-sentence" — phrases that aren't full sentences but function adverbially — and its two forms.

---

## What it is

**شبه الجملة** is a phrase that stands *in place of* a sentence (hence also called **ظرف**, because it occupies a sentence's slot via a deleted operator). It comes in two forms by structure:

| Form | Arabic | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Prepositional phrase | **الجارّ والمجرور** | في مكةَ (in Makkah) |
| Adverb + genitive | **الظرف والمضاف إليه** | عند الكعبةِ (by the Kaʿbah) |

> ⚠️ The word **ظرف** has three senses: (1) the **مفعول فيه** (the adverbial object of time/place); (2) the specific words **فوق، تحت، عند** and their sisters; (3) the **شبه الجملة** as a whole.

> *Section Two: On the quasi-sentence (shibh al-jumlah).*

![[n12-l2.png]]
> *The quasi-sentence is [also] called a **ẓarf** (adverbial).*

![[n12-l3.png]]
> *The word "ẓarf" is applied to several senses: the first, the **مفعول فيه**; the second, the words **fawqa, taḥta, ʿinda** and their sisters; the third, the **quasi-sentence**.*

![[n12-l4.png]]
> *And in it are two issues.*

![[n12-l5.png]]
> *Issue One: the kinds of quasi-sentence with respect to its **form**.*

![[n12-l6.png]]
> *It is of two kinds:*

![[n12-l7.png]]
> *The first kind: the **prepositional phrase** (al-jārr wa-l-majrūr) — e.g. **fī Makkah** (in Makkah).*

![[n12-l8.png]]
> *The second kind: the **adverb with its genitive** (al-ẓarf wa-l-muḍāf ilayhi) — e.g. **ʿinda l-Kaʿbah** (by the Kaʿbah).*
