# 25 — أفعالُ التحويل (Transformation Verbs)

> **Video:** [أفعال التحويل](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UyfyUhqQyuw)
> **Book:** p.27
> **Topic:** The second branch of two-object verbs whose objects were originally a مبتدأ+خبر — these mean **"to make/turn X into Y"** (بمعنى صَيَّر).

---

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.27

![[n25-l1.png]]
> *The **transformation verbs** (afʿāl at-taḥwīl) are those meaning **ṣayyara** (to make/turn into), namely:*

![[n25-l2.png]]
> ***ṣayyara** — "ṣayyara ʿadhba sh-sharābi murrā" (he turned the sweet drink bitter) / "wa-zāda ḍīqa l-ḥayāti ḍīqā" (and increased life's tightness).*

![[n25-l3.png]]
> ***radda** — ﴿wadda kathīrun min ahli l-kitābi law **yaruddūnakum** min baʿdi īmānikum kuffārā﴾ (they wish to turn you back into disbelievers).*

![[n25-l4.png]]
> ***taraka** — ﴿fa-**tarakahu** ṣaldā﴾ (and leaves it bare).*

![[n25-l5.png]]
> ***takhadha / ittakhadha** — ﴿wa-**ttakhadha**-llāhu Ibrāhīma khalīlā﴾ (and Allah took Ibrāhīm as an intimate friend).*

![[n25-l6.png]]
> ***jaʿala** — ﴿fa-**jaʿalnāhu** habāʾan manthūrā﴾ (and We made it scattered dust).*

![[n25-l7.png]]
> ***wahaba** — "wahabaniya-llāhu fidāʾan li-l-ʿilm" (may Allah make me a ransom for knowledge).*

![[n25-l8.png]]
> *These verbs do **not** take two objects unless used **as transformation verbs** — e.g.:*

![[n25-l9.png]]
> *﴿innī **taraktu** millata qawmin lā yuʾminūna bi-llāh﴾ (I have left the religion of a people who do not believe) — here taraka = *abandon* (one object).*

![[n25-l10.png]]
> *﴿thumma **yuraddu** ilā rabbih﴾ (then he is returned to his Lord) — i.e. *yuṣār* (is brought back).*

![[n25-l11.png]]
> *﴿wa-**jaʿala** lakumu s-samʿa wa-l-abṣāra wa-l-afʾidah﴾ (and He made for you hearing, sight, and hearts) — i.e. *khalaqa/awjada* (created), one object.*
