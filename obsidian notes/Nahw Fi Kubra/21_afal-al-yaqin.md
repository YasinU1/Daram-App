# 21 — أفعالُ اليقين (Verbs of Certainty)

> **Video:** [أفعال اليقين](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fyFR59FQBcs)
> **Book:** أفعال القلوب — القسم الأول: اليقين (p.24–25)
> **Topic:** The first group of [[20_al-mutaaddi-ila-mafulayn|heart-verbs]] — those that put their two objects (an original مبتدأ+خبر) under the meaning of **certain knowledge**.

---

These verbs take **two objects** that were originally a subject + predicate, and assert the content as **known for certain**.

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.24–25

![[n21-l1.png]]
> *The first [group]: those denoting **certainty** (al-yaqīn), namely:*

![[n21-l2.png]]
> ***wajada** (to find/realize) — ﴿wa-in **wajadnā** aktharahum la-fāsiqīn﴾ (indeed We found most of them defiantly disobedient).*

![[n21-l3.png]]
> ***alfā** (to find) — ﴿**alfaw** ābāʾahum ḍāllīn﴾ (they found their fathers astray).*

![[n21-l4.png]]
> ***raʾā** (of the heart — to deem/know) — ﴿innahum **yarawnahu** baʿīdan wa-**narāhu** qarīban﴾ (they see it far, We see it near).*

![[n21-l5.png]]
> ***ʿalima** (to know) — ﴿wa-**yaʿlamūna** annahā l-ḥaqq﴾ (and they know it is the truth).*

![[n21-l6.png]]
> ***ʿarafa** (to know/recognize) — **ʿaraftu** Muḥammadan ﷺ nabiyyā (I knew Muhammad ﷺ to be a prophet).*

![[n21-l7.png]]
> ***darā** (to know) — ﴿wa-mā **adrī** mā yufʿalu bī wa-lā bikum﴾ (I know not what will be done with me or you).*

![[n21-l8.png]]
> ***taʿallam** (= "know!") — **taʿallam** shifāʾa n-nafsi qahra ʿaduwwihā (Know! the soul's cure is subduing its enemy).*
