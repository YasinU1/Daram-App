# 14 — الكلامُ المُوجَبُ وغيرُ المُوجَب (Affirmative & Non-affirmative Speech)

> **Video:** [الكلام الموجب وغير الموجب](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgYln2gXVL8)
> **Book:** الفصل الثالث في الكلام (p.16)
> **Topic:** Defines **الكلام** (meaningful, self-sufficient speech) and divides it by whether it is **affirmed** or carries a negation/prohibition/question.

---

## الكلام vs الجملة

> **الكلامُ: قولٌ مُفيدٌ** — *meaningful speech upon which silence is good* (يَحسُنُ السكوتُ عليه).
- **صَدَقْتَ** ("you spoke truly") is كلام — you can stop there.
- **إنْ تَصدُقْ** ("if you speak truly") is **not** كلام — it leaves you hanging.

> **Key relation:** **every كلام is a جملة, but not every جملة is كلام.** (A جملة only needs مسند + مسند إليه; كلام must also be *complete in sense*.)

## The two kinds

| | المُوجَب (affirmative) | غير المُوجَب (non-affirmative) |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | has **no** negation, prohibition, or question | contains a **نفي / نهي / استفهام** |
| Example | ﴿أعبُدُ اللهَ الذي يتوفّاكم﴾ | نفي: ﴿فلا أعبُدُ...﴾ · نهي: ﴿لا تُشرِكْ بالله﴾ · استفهام: ﴿أتتخذ من دونه آلهة﴾ |

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.16

![[n14-l1.png]]
> *Section Three: On speech (al-kalām).*

![[n14-l2.png]]
> *It is **meaningful speech** (qawl mufīd).*

![[n14-l3.png]]
> *The "meaningful" is that upon which silence is good — e.g. **ṣadaqta** (you spoke truly) — unlike "**in taṣduq**" (if you speak truly);*

![[n14-l4.png]]
> *so **every kalām is a jumlah, but not every jumlah is kalām**.*

![[n14-l5.png]]
> *And it is of two kinds:*

![[n14-l6.png]]
> *The first kind: the **affirmative** (al-mūjab).*

![[n14-l7.png]]
> *It is that which has no negation, prohibition, or question — e.g. ﴿aʿbudu-llāha lladhī yatawaffākum﴾ (I worship Allah who takes your souls).*

![[n14-l8.png]]
> *The second kind: the **non-affirmative** (ghayr al-mūjab).*

![[n14-l9.png]]
> *It is that which contains:*

![[n14-l10.png]]
> *a **negation** — e.g. ﴿fa-lā aʿbudu lladhīna taʿbudūna min dūni-llāh﴾ (so I do not worship those you worship besides Allah);*

![[n14-l11.png]]
> *or a **prohibition** — e.g. ﴿lā tushrik bi-llāh﴾ (do not associate [partners] with Allah);*

![[n14-l12.png]]
> *or an **interrogation** — e.g. ﴿a-tattakhidhu min dūnihi ālihah﴾ (do you take gods besides Him?).*
