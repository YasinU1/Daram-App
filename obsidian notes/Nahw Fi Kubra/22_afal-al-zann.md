# 22 — أفعالُ الظنّ (Verbs of Supposition / Preponderant Opinion)

> **Video:** [أفعال الظن](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41Uo2WYCG7g)
> **Book:** أفعال القلوب — القسم الثاني: الظن (p.25)
> **Topic:** The second group of [[20_al-mutaaddi-ila-mafulayn|heart-verbs]] — those asserting the content as **likely/preponderant** rather than certain. Also called **أفعال الرُّجحان**.

---

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.25

![[n22-l1.png]]
> *The second: those denoting **preponderant opinion** (aẓ-ẓann), also called **afʿāl ar-rujḥān**, namely:*

![[n22-l2.png]]
> ***ẓanna** (to think) — ﴿alladhīna **yaẓunnūna** annahum mulāqū rabbihim wa-annahum ilayhi rājiʿūn﴾ (who think they will meet their Lord and to Him return).*

![[n22-l3.png]]
> ***ḥasiba** (to reckon) — ﴿wa-**yaḥsabūna** annahum muhtadūn﴾ (and they reckon themselves guided).*

![[n22-l4.png]]
> ***zaʿama** (to claim) — ﴿**zaʿama** lladhīna kafarū an lan yubʿathū﴾ (those who disbelieved claimed they would not be resurrected).*

![[n22-l5.png]]
> ***jaʿala** (to deem) — ﴿wa-**jaʿalū** l-malāʾikata lladhīna hum ʿibādu r-raḥmāni ināthā﴾ (and they deemed the angels … to be females).*

![[n22-l6.png]]
> *and also **ḥajā · ʿadda · wahaba · khāla** — all of the same (ẓann) group.*

---

> **Tip:** these same verbs can sometimes carry **certainty** in context (e.g. ẓanna meaning *to be sure*), but their **default** is preponderant opinion — hence *afʿāl ar-rujḥān*.
