# 17 — الإعراب (Iʿrāb)

> **Video:** [الإعراب](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i1v1zxfrf0c)
> **Book:** الفصل الثالث في الإعراب (p.18–19)
> **Topic:** Closes Chapter Three — what iʿrāb *is* (the operator's effect on word-endings) and its three kinds.

---

## Definition

> **الإعراب: أثرٌ يَجلِبُه العاملُ في آخرِ الكلمةِ.**

The **effect** an [[15_al-amil-wa-ghayr-al-amil|operator]] produces at the **end of a word** — i.e. the change of ending (raf ʿ / naṣb / jarr / jazm).

> *Why iʿrāb matters (book fāʾidah, p.18): it has three benefits — **الإظهار** (showing meanings through the wording), **إزالة الفساد/الالتباس** (removing ambiguity), and **التحسين** (beautifying the wording).*

## The three kinds

| Kind | Arabic | What | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **اللفظي** (overt) | the marker **appears** | يجتهدُ المؤمنُ في الخيرِ |
| 2 | **التقديري** (estimated) | marker hidden by an **obstacle** (مانع) | يسعى الفتى إلى الهدى (on الفتى/الهدى) |
| 3 | **المحلّي** (positional) | word **can't bear** a marker (indeclinable), so it holds the *position* | تسعينَ، أنتنَّ، إليه |

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.18–19

![[n17-l1.png]]
> *Section Three: On iʿrāb.*

![[n17-l2.png]]
> *It is an **effect** that the operator brings about at the **end of the word**.*

![[n17-l3.png]]
> *And it is of three kinds:*

![[n17-l4.png]]
> *The first kind: **overt** iʿrāb (al-lafẓī).*

![[n17-l5.png]]
> *It is that which **appears** in pronunciation — like the ḍamma and kasra in "yajtahidu l-muʾminu fī l-khayr" (the believer strives in good).*

![[n17-l6.png]]
> *The second kind: **estimated** iʿrāb (at-taqdīrī).*

![[n17-l7.png]]
> *It is that which does **not appear** due to an obstacle — like the estimated ḍamma and kasra in "yasʿā l-fatā ilā l-hudā" (the youth strives toward guidance).*

![[n17-l8.png]]
> *The third kind: **positional** iʿrāb (al-maḥallī).*

![[n17-l9.png]]
> *It is that which does not appear because the word cannot bear an iʿrāb-marker [it is indeclinable] — e.g. tisʿīn, antunna, ilayh.*
