# 08 — أركان الجملة (The Pillars of the Sentence)

> **Video:** [أركان الجملة](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A6aB03d68xU)
> **Book:** الباب الثاني، الفصل الأول في الجملة (p.10)
> **Topic:** Opens **Chapter Two** (sentence / quasi-sentence / speech). A جملة = a **مسند** + a **مسند إليه**, and the vocabulary for its parts.

---

## Core idea

> **الجملةُ: ما اشتملت على مُسنَدٍ ومُسنَدٍ إليه.**

Every sentence is a **predication**: you *attribute* something (the مُسنَد) *to* something (the مُسنَد إليه).

| Term | What it is | Also called |
|---|---|---|
| **المُسنَد إليه** | the مبتدأ (subject of nominal sentence) or the فاعل (doer) | **محكوم عليه** (that which is judged about) |
| **المُسنَد** | the خبر (predicate) or the فعل (verb) | **محكوم به** (that which is judged with) |

- Together, the مسند + مسند إليه are the **عُمدة** — the indispensable core.
- Everything else (objects, adverbs, etc.) is **فَضْلة** (surplus) and **قَيْد** (restriction).

> This frames the whole grammar: العُمدة can't be dropped; the فَضْلة can.

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.10 (أركان الجملة)

![[n08-l1.png]]
> *Chapter Two: On the sentence (al-jumlah), the quasi-sentence (shibh al-jumlah), and speech (al-kalām).*

![[n08-l2.png]]
> *And in it are three sections.*

![[n08-l3.png]]
> *Section One: On the sentence.*

![[n08-l4.png]]
> *It is that which comprises a **musnad** (predicate) and a **musnad ilayhi** (that to which it is predicated).*

![[n08-l5.png]]
> *The **musnad ilayhi** is the mubtadaʾ or the fāʿil; it is called "that which is judged about" (maḥkūm ʿalayhi).*

![[n08-l6.png]]
> *The **musnad** is the khabar or the fiʿl; it is called "that which is judged with" (maḥkūm bihi).*

![[n08-l7.png]]
> *The musnad ilayhi and the musnad are [together] called **ʿumdah** (the core); whatever is besides them is called **faḍlah** (surplus) and **qayd** (restriction).*
