# 01 — قَبْلَ الكِتابِ (Before the Book)

> **Series:** Al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw (الكُبْرى في النَّحْوِ)
> **Video:** [قبل الكتاب](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZC8N2PnDzQ)
> **Type:** Author's preface — orientation, not yet grammar content.

---

## What this book is

- **الكُبْرى في النَّحْوِ** ("The Greater [book] in Grammar") is the **advanced/comprehensive** level in a graded series. It builds on two earlier, simpler primers — **الصُّغْرى** (aṣ-Ṣughrā, "the smaller") and **الوُسْطى** (al-Wusṭā, "the middle") — which were originally separate books but are now **merged into one print**, with *al-Kubrā* layered on top.
- Same **methodology, chapter order (الأبْوابُ), headings, and structure** as the earlier primers — but with the **advanced material** an upper-level student needs added in.
- Originally written by the teacher as **his own personal notes** (مُذَكِّراتٌ), then opened up for other students to benefit from.

## Sources it draws on

Compiled from the major works of the grammarians (كُتُبُ النَّحْوِ). The book's own preface names them explicitly: **أَلْفِيَّةُ ابنِ مالِكٍ**, **أَوْضَحُ المَسالِكِ**, **هِدايَةُ النَّحْوِ**, **الكافِيَةُ**, and tafsīr works like **الجَلالَيْنِ** and **البَيْضاويِّ / التَّحْريرِ والتَّنْويرِ**.

## The defining aim: naḥw **for the Qurʾān**

This is the key distinguishing feature of the book — stated verbatim in the preface (*"وكانَ جُلَّ اهْتِمامي غَوامِضَ آياتِ القُرآنِ وإعْرابَ القُرآنِ… كأنَّهُ مُقَدِّمَةٌ لُغَوِيَّةٌ لِتَفْسيرِ القُرآنِ"*):

- Standard naḥw texts leave out many **مَسائِلَ** (issues) that you meet when reading the **Qurʾān**, or mention them only in passing.
- So whenever a Qurʾānic construction needs a grammar point the usual texts skip, **this book adds it in**.
- Framing: the book is effectively a **linguistic introduction to the Qurʾān (مُقَدِّمَةٌ لُغَوِيَّةٌ لِتَفْسيرِ القُرآنِ)** — it prepares you to understand *how the language of the Qurʾān works*.

## Method / style choices

- **Clear text over terse text:** chooses explicit explanation rather than the dense classical-matn style. (*"وآثَرْتُ تَوْضيحَ العِبارَةِ على إيجازِها"*.)
- **Examples everywhere (الأمْثِلَةُ):** prefers concrete examples — wherever possible **Qurʾānic verses (آياتٌ)** — over abstraction (*"وإيرادَ الأمْثِلَةِ على الاعْتِمادِ على فَهْمِ قارِئِها"*).
- Intended as a **reference** you can return to.

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.4 (المُقَدِّمَةُ)

The book's written preface, line by line, with translation:

![[n01-l1.png]]
> *In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate.*

![[n01-l2.png]]
> *All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds; and prayer and peace be upon our master Muḥammad ﷺ, his family, and all his companions.*

![[n01-l3.png]]
> *To proceed: this is a treatise [arranged] in an introduction (مُقَدِّمَةٌ), three objectives (ثلاثَةُ مَقاصِدَ), and a conclusion (خاتِمَةٌ).*

**The statement of purpose** (footnote — the heart of the preface):

![[n01-preface.png]]
> *This is a comprehensive [work] in grammar that I have named **al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw**. I took it up as a reminder for myself and for whoever of the brethren wishes [to use it] after me. In it I followed what I followed in **al-Wusṭā**, adding what the advanced student needs — issues I gleaned from the books of the masters of the field, such as the **Alfiyyah of Ibn Mālik**, **Awḍaḥ al-Masālik**, **Hidāyat an-Naḥw**, **al-Kāfiyah**, and others; and what I gathered from the books of tafsīr and iʿrāb of the Qurʾān, such as **Tafsīr al-Jalālayn**, **al-Bayḍāwī**, and **at-Taḥrīr wa-t-Tanwīr** — for the bulk of my concern was the subtleties of the verses of the Qurʾān and its iʿrāb, as though it were a linguistic introduction to the tafsīr of the Qurʾān. And I preferred clarifying the wording over abridging it, and citing examples over reliance on [merely] its reader's understanding.*

## Key terms introduced

| Arabic | Translit. | Meaning here |
|---|---|---|
| النَّحْوُ | an-naḥw | Arabic grammar / syntax |
| الكُبْرى / الوُسْطى / الصُّغْرى | al-Kubrā / al-Wusṭā / aṣ-Ṣughrā | the graded levels (greater / middle / smaller) |
| البابُ (ج الأبْوابُ) | al-bāb | a chapter / topic-section |
| المَسْأَلَةُ (ج المَسائِلُ) | al-masʾalah | a specific grammatical issue/question |
| المَتْنُ | al-matn | the base text being explained |

## Takeaway

A scene-setting preface. No grammar yet — but it fixes the **lens** for the whole series: every rule is taught with an eye to **reading and understanding the Qurʾān**. The book then opens with **المُقَدِّمَةُ → البابُ الأوّلُ في الكَلِمَةِ**.
