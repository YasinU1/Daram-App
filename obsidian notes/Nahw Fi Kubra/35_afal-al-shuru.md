# 35 — أفعالُ الشروع (Verbs of Onset / Beginning)

> **Video:** [أفعال الشروع](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xOM7eLD4yE)
> **Book:** الأفعال الناقصة — الضرب الرابع: أفعال الشروع (p.36)
> **Topic:** The fourth and last category — verbs meaning **to begin/set about** doing something.

---

## The verbs

> **الضربُ الرابعُ: أفعالُ الشروعِ** — *ما وُضِعَت للدلالةِ على الشروعِ في الفعلِ.*
> Among them: **جَعَلَ، أخَذَ، طَفِقَ، عَلِقَ، أنشأَ**.
> - ﴿و**طَفِقا** يَخصِفانِ عليهما من وَرَقِ الجنّةِ﴾ — *"and they began to fasten over themselves the leaves of the Garden."*

## مسائل
- **قياسية لا سماعية:** unlike the other deficient-verb categories, أفعال الشروع are **rule-governed (قياسية)**; they're listed here only for their **resemblance** to the deficient verbs in operation.
- **Form & خبر:** they occur **only in the past tense**, and their خبر must be a **verbal sentence in the present (مضارعية)**, **bare of أنْ** (since beginning ≠ futurity).
- **عسى/كاد/طفق & sisters** are sometimes all grouped together loosely as **أفعال المقاربة**.

> **Why no أنْ?** أنْ implies *futurity*, but "beginning" an act means it is **already underway** — so its present-tense خبر cannot take أنْ. (Contrast the hope/proximity verbs above.)
