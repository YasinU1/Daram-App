# 28 — أصبحَ وأضحى وأمسى وظلَّ وباتَ

> **Video:** [أصبح وأضحى وأمسى وظل وبات](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Os7bVPI6ZE)
> **Book:** كان وأخواتها — الثاني–السادس (p.31)
> **Topic:** Five sisters of [[27_kana|كان]] that attach the خبر to the اسم **at a particular time of day**.

---

Each means "[the noun] *came to be / was* [the predicate] **at** [a time]":

| # | Verb | Time | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **أصبَحَ** | morning (الصباح) | ﴿فأصبَحَ في المدينةِ خائفًا يَترقَّبُ﴾ |
| 3 | **أضحى** | forenoon (الضحى) | أضحى الرجلُ عامِلًا |
| 4 | **أمسى** | evening (المساء) | أمسى الرجلُ كالًّا |
| 5 | **ظلَّ** | daytime (الظِّل = النهار) | ﴿وانظُرْ إلى إلهِكَ الذي ظَلْتَ عليه عاكِفًا﴾ |
| 6 | **باتَ** | night (المبيت = الليل) | ﴿والذين يَبيتونَ لربِّهم سُجَّدًا وقِيامًا﴾ |

> **غدا بمعنى أصبح:** ﴿وغَدَوْا على حَرْدٍ قادرين﴾. · **ظَلْتَ:** originally *ẓaliltَ* — one of the two lāms is dropped.

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.31

![[n28-l1.png]]
> *The second: **aṣbaḥa** — for the noun's being described by the predicate **in the morning** — e.g. ﴿fa-aṣbaḥa fī l-madīnati khāʾifan yataraqqab﴾ (and he became, in the city, fearful and vigilant).*

![[n28-l2.png]]
> *The third: **aḍḥā** — at the **forenoon** — e.g. aḍḥā r-rajulu ʿāmilan (the man became, at forenoon, working).*

![[n28-l3.png]]
> *The fourth: **amsā** — in the **evening** — e.g. amsā r-rajulu kāllan (the man became, by evening, weary).*

![[n28-l4.png]]
> *The fifth: **ẓalla** — in the **daytime** (the ẓill = the day) — e.g. ﴿wa-nẓur ilā ilāhika lladhī ẓalta ʿalayhi ʿākifā﴾ (and look at your "god" to which you remained devoted).*

![[n28-l5.png]]
> *The sixth: **bāta** — at **night** (al-mabīt = the night) — e.g. ﴿wa-lladhīna yabītūna li-rabbihim sujjadan wa-qiyāmā﴾ (and those who spend the night before their Lord, prostrate and standing).*
