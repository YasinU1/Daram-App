# 26 — الفعلُ المتعدّي إلى ثلاثةِ مفاعيل (Transitive to Three Objects)

> **Video:** [الفعل المتعدي إلى ثلاثة مفاعيل](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XzjczuKoP6U)
> **Book:** الضرب الثالث (p.27)
> **Topic:** The third and highest degree of transitivity — verbs taking **three** objects.

---

## Core rule

> A three-object verb is **only ever a heart-verb** (in its causative form). Its **2nd and 3rd** objects are the original **مبتدأ + خبر**; the **1st** object is the new perceiver introduced by the causative.

**Structure:** `أعلمَ [1st = who is informed] [2nd + 3rd = the original مبتدأ+خبر]`

**Verbs:** the causatives of perception/knowledge — **أَرَى، أَعْلَمَ، نَبَّأَ، أَنبأَ، خَبَّرَ، أَخبَرَ، حَدَّثَ**.

## 📖 من الكتاب — p.27

![[n26-l1.png]]
> *The third category: the verb transitive to **three** objects.*

![[n26-l2.png]]
> *It is **only** a heart-verb [in causative form]; its 2nd and 3rd objects were originally a subject + predicate — e.g. ﴿kadhālika **yurīhimu**-llāhu aʿmālahum ḥasarātin ʿalayhim﴾ (thus Allah will show them their deeds as regrets upon them).*

> In ﴿yurīhim … aʿmālahum ḥasarāt﴾: **-him** = 1st object (those shown), **aʿmālahum** = 2nd, **ḥasarāt** = 3rd — and *aʿmālahum ḥasarātٌ* was originally a mubtadaʾ + khabar ("their deeds [are] regrets").
