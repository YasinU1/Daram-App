# 15 — العاملُ وغيرُ العامل (Operators & Non-operators)

> **Video:** [العامل وغير العامل](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=boLFYQ5A0go)
> **Book:** الباب الثالث، الفصل الأول في العامل وغير العامل (p.17)
> **Topic:** Opens **Chapter Three** (عامل / معمول / إعراب). The **عامل** is what *causes* case/mood; this lesson sorts the word-classes into operators vs non-operators.

---

## Core idea

> **العاملُ: ما يوجبُ حالةً** — that which *necessitates a state* (a case or mood) in another word.
> **غيرُ العامل: ما لا يوجبُ حالةً** — that which does not. (Also called **عاطل** / **مُهمَل**.)

- Operator example: **لَنْ** in ﴿ولَنْ نُشرِكَ بربِّنا أحدًا﴾ — it forces the verb into **naṣb**.
- Non-operator example: **ما** in ﴿ما أشرَكْنا﴾ — it changes nothing.

## Who operates? (book summary table, p.17)

| Class | Operator? |
|---|---|
| **الأفعال** (verbs) | **all** of them |
| **الحروف** (particles) | **some** |
| **الأسماء** (nouns) | **some** |
| **الجملة** (sentences) | none — all are non-operators |
| **شبه الجملة** (quasi-sentences) | none — all are non-operators |

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.17

![[n15-l1.png]]
> *Chapter Three: On the operator (al-ʿāmil), the operated-upon (al-maʿmūl), and iʿrāb.*

![[n15-l2.png]]
> *And it contains three sections.*

![[n15-l3.png]]
> *Section One: On the operator and the non-operator.*

![[n15-l4.png]]
> *The **operator** is that which **necessitates a state** — e.g. **lan** in ﴿wa-lan nushrika bi-rabbinā aḥadā﴾ (it puts the verb in naṣb).*

![[n15-l5.png]]
> *The operator is:*

![[n15-l6.png]]
> ***all** the verbs;*

![[n15-l7.png]]
> *and **some** of the particles;*

![[n15-l8.png]]
> *and **some** of the nouns.*

![[n15-l9.png]]
> *The **non-operator** is that which does not necessitate a state — e.g. **mā** in ﴿mā ashraknā﴾ (We associated nothing).*

![[n15-l10.png]]
> *The non-operator is:*

![[n15-l11.png]]
> ***some** of the particles;*

![[n15-l12.png]]
> *and **some** of the nouns;*

![[n15-l13.png]]
> *and **all** the sentences;*

![[n15-l14.png]]
> *and **all** the quasi-sentences.*

> *(The non-operator is also called **عاطِل** and **مُهمَل**.)*
