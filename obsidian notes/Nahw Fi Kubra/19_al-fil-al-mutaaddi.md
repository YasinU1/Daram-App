# 19 — الفعلُ المتعدّي (The Transitive Verb)

> **Video:** [الفعل المتعدي](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIpb-wGbtpE)
> **Book:** القسم الثاني: الفعل المتعدي (p.23)
> **Topic:** The other half of the قياسي verb — one whose effect **reaches an object** — how it reaches it, and its three degrees of transitivity.

---

## Definition & names

> **المتعدي: ما يتجاوزُ أثرُ فاعلِه إلى المفعولِ به.**
- Also called **فعل واقع** (falling upon its object) and **فعل مجاوز** (whose effect passes over).

## How it reaches the object
- **بنفسه** (directly): الذي **أذهبَ** عنا الحزن.
- **بواسطة حرف جر** (via a preposition): ذهبَ اللهُ **بـ**نورهم. → then the object is a *non-explicit object* (مفعول به غير صريح).
- A **single verb** can do both: **اهدنا** الصراط (direct) vs **هداه إلى** صراط (via حرف).

## Three degrees of transitivity
1. **إلى مفعول واحد** — one object: ﴿خلق السمواتِ والأرضَ﴾.
2. **[[20_al-mutaaddi-ila-mafulayn|إلى مفعولين]]** — two objects (next lesson).
3. **إلى ثلاثة مفاعيل** — three objects (e.g. أرى، أعلم).

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.23

![[n19-l1.png]]
> *The second kind: the **transitive verb** (al-fiʿl al-mutaʿaddī).*

![[n19-l2.png]]
> *It is that whose effect **passes beyond its doer to an object**.*

![[n19-l3.png]]
> *The transitive verb is [also] called a **fiʿl wāqiʿ** (falling upon its object) and a **fiʿl mujāwiz** (whose effect passes over to the object).*

![[n19-l4.png]]
> *A verb may be transitive:*

![[n19-l5.png]]
> ***by itself** (directly) — e.g. ﴿al-ḥamdu li-llāhi lladhī **adhhaba** ʿannā l-ḥazan﴾ (Praise to Allah who removed grief from us);*

![[n19-l6.png]]
> *or **via a preposition** — e.g. ﴿**dhahaba**-llāhu **bi**-nūrihim﴾ (Allah took away their light).*

![[n19-l7.png]]
> *In that case the object is called "an object via a preposition," or a "mutaʿallaq in the sense of an object," or a "non-explicit object."*

![[n19-l8.png]]
> *A single verb may be:*

![[n19-l9.png]]
> ***sometimes** transitive by itself — e.g. ﴿**ihdinā** ṣ-ṣirāṭa l-mustaqīm﴾ (Guide us to the straight path);*

![[n19-l10.png]]
> *and **sometimes** transitive via a preposition — e.g. ﴿wa-**hadaynāhu ilā** ṣirāṭin mustaqīm﴾ (and We guided him to a straight path).*

![[n19-l11.png]]
> *The transitive verb is of **three categories**:*

![[n19-l12.png]]
> *The first: transitive to **one** object.*

![[n19-l13.png]]
> *e.g. ﴿**khalaqa** s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ﴾ (He created the heavens and the earth).*
