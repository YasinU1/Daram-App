# 33 — كادَ (and the Verbs of Proximity, أفعال المقاربة)

> **Video:** [كاد](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QHD0pbKFkw8)
> **Book:** الضرب الثالث: أفعال المقاربة — كاد (p.35)
> **Topic:** The third category of deficient verbs — set for the **nearness of the predicate's occurrence (قرب وقوع الخبر)**. The chief of them is **كاد**.

---

## كاد

> **الضربُ الثالثُ: أفعالُ المقاربةِ** — *ما وُضِعَت للدلالةِ على قُربِ وقوعِ الخبرِ.*
> **الأوّل: كادَ** — ﴿و**كادوا** يَقتُلونَني﴾ (they almost killed me).

- عسى vs. كاد: both carry **مقاربة**, but عسى is on the path of **hope (الرجاء)**, while **كاد and its sisters** are on the path of **actual nearing (الحصول)**.

## كاد المنفيّ — the famous puzzle

| Form | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **كاد** (affirmative) | **negates** the act's occurrence, **with** nearness to it — "almost did, but didn't" |
| **ما كاد / لا يكاد** (negative) | **negates** the *nearness*, **with** the act's occurrence — even **more** emphatic in negation |

> ﴿ويُسقى من ماءٍ صديدٍ … ولا **يكادُ** يُسيغُه﴾ — he can scarcely swallow it (the negative *yakādu* is the strongest denial of swallowing).

> *Note (fāʾidah):* occurrence can be understood **after** negating the nearness — ﴿فذَبَحوها وما **كادوا** يَفعَلون﴾ (they slaughtered it, though they had almost not done so).
