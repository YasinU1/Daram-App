# 16 — المعمولُ وغيرُ المعمول (Operated-upon & Non-operated-upon)

> **Video:** [المعمول وغير المعمول](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jgi0g8ovJAQ)
> **Book:** الفصل الثاني في المعمول وغير المعمول (p.17–18)
> **Topic:** The flip-side of [[15_al-amil-wa-ghayr-al-amil|the operator]] — which words can **receive** a case/mood (and thus have iʿrāb), and which cannot.

---

## Core idea

> **المعمول: ما يَقبَلُ حالةً ويكونُ له إعرابٌ** — that which *accepts a state* and **has iʿrāb**.
> **غير المعمول: ما لا يَقبَلُ حالةً ولا يكونُ له إعرابٌ.**

## Who is operated-upon? (book summary, p.17–18)

| Class | maʿmūl? |
|---|---|
| **الأسماء** (nouns) | **all** — except أسماء الفعل (verbal-nouns) |
| **الفعل** | the مضارع, and the ماضٍ that a **ناصب/جازم** entered |
| **الجملة** | **some** |
| **شبه الجملة** | **all** |
| **الحروف** | none (always non-operated) |
| أسماء الفعل · ماضٍ without ناصب/جازم | non-operated |

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.17–18

![[n16-l1.png]]
> *Section Two: On the operated-upon (al-maʿmūl) and the non-operated-upon.*

![[n16-l2.png]]
> *The **maʿmūl** is that which accepts a state and has iʿrāb — e.g. **ṣāḥib** in ﴿mā ḍalla ṣāḥibukum﴾ (your companion has not strayed).*

![[n16-l3.png]]
> *The maʿmūl is:*

![[n16-l4.png]]
> ***all** the nouns **except** the verbal-nouns (asmāʾ al-fiʿl);*

![[n16-l5.png]]
> *and the present verb, and the past verb that a **nāṣib or jāzim** entered;*

![[n16-l6.png]]
> *and **some** of the sentences;*

![[n16-l7.png]]
> *and **all** the quasi-sentences.*

![[n16-l8.png]]
> *The **non-maʿmūl** is that which accepts no state and has no iʿrāb — e.g. **mā** in ﴿mā ḍalla ṣāḥibukum﴾.*

![[n16-l9.png]]
> *The non-maʿmūl is:*

![[n16-l10.png]]
> *the verbal-nouns (asmāʾ al-fiʿl);*

![[n16-l11.png]]
> *and the past verb that no nāṣib or jāzim entered;*

![[n16-l12.png]]
> *and **all** the particles;*

![[n16-l13.png]]
> *and **some** of the sentences.*
