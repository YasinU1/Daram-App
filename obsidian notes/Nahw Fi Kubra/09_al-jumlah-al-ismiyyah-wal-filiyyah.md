# 09 — الجملةُ الاسميةُ والفعلية (Nominal & Verbal Sentences)

> **Video:** [الجملة الاسمية والفعلية](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oi42RjpRWmY)
> **Book:** المسألة الأولى: أقسام الجملة باعتبار هيئتها (p.11)
> **Topic:** The first of three divisions of the sentence — by its **form**: does it *begin* with a noun or a verb?

---

## The division (by form / هيئة)

| | الجملة الاسمية | الجملة الفعلية |
|---|---|---|
| Begins with | a **noun** (اسم) | a **verb** (فعل) |
| Example | ﴿واللهُ غفورٌ رحيم﴾ | ﴿وخَلَقَ كلَّ شيءٍ﴾ |

The **nominal** sentence further splits by whether a **نَاسِخ** (abrogator — kāna & sisters, inna & sisters) has entered:
- **غير المنسوخة** (un-abrogated): no nāsikh — ﴿واللهُ غفورٌ رحيم﴾.
- **المنسوخة** (abrogated): a nāsikh entered — ﴿**إنَّ** اللهَ غفورٌ رحيم﴾.

> **Two clarifications from the book (masāʾil, p.11):**
> 1. A **nāsikh or a faḍlah does not change** whether a sentence counts as nominal/verbal (on the soundest view) — it's judged by its true opening element: ﴿ما هذا بَشَرًا﴾ and ﴿وكان اللهُ عليمًا حكيمًا﴾ are **اسمية**; ﴿إياك نعبد﴾ is **فعلية**.
> 2. The **conditional sentence (الشرطية) is verbal**, because only verbs follow a شرط particle — ﴿إنْ تُخفُوا ما في صدوركم أو تُبدُوه يَعلَمْهُ الله﴾; and in ﴿وإنْ أحدٌ من المشركين استجارك﴾ a verb is **estimated** (تقدير: استجارك).

## 📖 من الكتاب — *al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw*, p.11

![[n09-l1.png]]
> *And in it [Section One] are three issues.*

![[n09-l2.png]]
> *Issue One: the kinds of sentence with respect to its **form**.*

![[n09-l3.png]]
> *It is of two kinds:*

![[n09-l4.png]]
> *The first kind: the **nominal sentence** (al-jumlah al-ismiyyah).*

![[n09-l5.png]]
> *It is that which **begins with a noun**; and it is two categories:*

![[n09-l6.png]]
> *The first category: the **un-abrogated** nominal sentence.*

![[n09-l7.png]]
> *It is that upon which no **nāsikh** has entered — e.g. ﴿wa-llāhu ghafūrun raḥīm﴾ (And Allah is Forgiving, Merciful).*

![[n09-l8.png]]
> *The second category: the **abrogated** nominal sentence.*

![[n09-l9.png]]
> *It is that upon which a nāsikh has entered — e.g. ﴿**inna**-llāha ghafūrun raḥīm﴾ (Indeed Allah is Forgiving, Merciful).*

![[n09-l10.png]]
> *The second kind: the **verbal sentence** (al-jumlah al-fiʿliyyah).*

![[n09-l11.png]]
> *It is that which **begins with a verb** — e.g. ﴿wa-**khalaqa** kulla shayʾ﴾ (And He created everything).*
