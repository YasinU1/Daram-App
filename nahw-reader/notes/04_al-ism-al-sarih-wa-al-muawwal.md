# 04 — الاسْمُ الصَّريحُ والمُؤَوَّل (The Explicit Noun & the Interpreted Noun)

> **Video:** [الاسم الصريح والمؤول](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z3_mL6YDGnA)
> **Topic:** Dividing the noun by **form** — is it a real single word, or is it a *construction* that stands **in the place of** a noun?
> *(Note: the spoken Arabic examples were dropped by YouTube's auto-captions; the standard examples are reconstructed below.)*

---

## The division

By its **wording/form**, an اسم is one of two kinds:

### 1. الاسْمُ الصَّريح (al-ism aṣ-ṣarīḥ) — *the explicit / outright noun*
A noun that is **a single, plain word** standing on its own.
- **زَيْدٌ، رَجُلٌ، الصِّيامُ، كِتابٌ** — each is openly, directly a noun.

### 2. الاسْمُ المُؤَوَّل (al-ism al-muʾawwal) — *the interpreted noun*
**Not** a single noun-word, but a **construction made of several words** that is *interpreted as* (تُؤَوَّل بـ) a noun and **takes the slot (مَحَلّ)** of one. Its core engine is the **المَصْدَر المُؤَوَّل** (the "interpreted verbal noun"):

> **حَرْف مَصْدَري + فِعْل  →  مَصْدَر (a noun)**

- **أَنْ + الفعل** → e.g. **أَنْ تَصُومُوا** → interpreted as the noun **صِيامُكُم**.
- (Also **أَنَّ + اسم + خبر** → a maṣdar, e.g. **أَنَّكَ قائمٌ** → **قِيامُكَ**.)

---

## Qurʾānic example (the one used in the lesson)

> **وَأَنْ تَصُومُوا خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ** — *“And that you fast is better for you.”*
> — سورة البقرة 2:184

- **أَنْ تَصُومُوا** = حرف مصدري (أنْ) + فعل (تصوموا).
- It is **interpreted as** the explicit noun **صِيامُكُمْ** ("your fasting").
- Reading: **وصِيامُكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ** — "**and your fasting** is better for you."
- Grammatically, that interpreted noun fills a real noun-slot here: it is the **مُبْتَدَأ** (subject), with **خَيْرٌ** as its **خَبَر**.

So an explicit noun (**صِيام**) and an interpreted construction (**أن تصوموا**) can do the **same syntactic job** — that's the whole point of the مؤوّل: it *behaves as* a noun even though it isn't one word.

---

## Side-by-side

| | الصَّريح (explicit) | المُؤَوَّل (interpreted) |
|---|---|---|
| Form | one plain noun-word | حرف مصدري + فعل (a construction) |
| Example | الصِّيامُ خَيْرٌ لَكُم | **أنْ تَصُومُوا** خَيْرٌ لَكُم |
| Status | *is* a noun | *stands in the place of* a noun (في مَحَلِّ اسمٍ) |
| Tool words | — | **أَنْ، أَنَّ، ما، كَيْ، لَوْ** (الأحرف المصدرية) |

---

## Why it matters for the Qurʾān
Qurʾānic sentences are full of **مصدر مؤوّل** sitting in subject/object/genitive positions. If you only look for a single noun-word, you'll miss the subject of a verse like 2:184. Training the eye to see **أنْ + فعل = a noun** is essential for correct iʿrāb of the Qurʾān.

> **Rule of thumb:** when you see **أنْ / أنَّ** + a clause, mentally swap in the **مصدر** (the "-ing" noun) and ask *what noun-slot is it filling?*
