# أحكام الاسم · فصل ٤ — التَّصْغير (The Diminutive)

> **Book:** al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw — الخَاتِمة, الباب الأول في أحكام الاسم, *al-Faṣl al-Rābiʿ fī al-taṣghīr*, **p. 216**.
> **Topic:** re-shaping a noun to signal **smallness** — the three diminutive templates.

---

## Definition
**التصغير**: *«تغييرُ بناءِ الاسمِ للدَّلالةِ على صِغَرِه»* — altering a noun's structure to mark it as small. Three صِيَغ:

| Template | For | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **فُعَيْل** | the **ثلاثي** | *نُجَيْم* (taṣghīr of *نَجْم*) |
| **فُعَيْعِل** | the **رباعي**, and the **خماسي** with **no مدّ letter before its last** | *دُرَيْهِم* (*دِرْهَم*) · *سُفَيْرِج* (*سَفَرْجَل*) |
| **فُعَيْعِيل** | the **خماسي with a مدّ letter before its last** | *مُفَيْتيح* (*مِفْتاح*) |

---

## Why it matters
Diminutive forms are fixed templates, not free — spotting *فُعَيْل / فُعَيْعِل / فُعَيْعِيل* tells you a word is a taṣghīr (smallness, endearment, or belittling) rather than a distinct root, and the third template is the tell that a مدّ letter sat before the last radical. Short فصل; pairs with [[ahkam-ism_5_nisba]] as the two "re-shaping" chapters.
