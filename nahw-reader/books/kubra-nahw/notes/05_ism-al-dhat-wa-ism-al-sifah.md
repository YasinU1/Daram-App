# 05 — اسْمُ الذّاتِ واسْمُ الصِّفة (Noun of Essence & Noun of Attribute)

> **Video:** [اسم ذات واسم صفة](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ITTG6jPhjnM)
> **Topic:** Dividing the noun by its **meaning** (لا by its form) — does it name a *thing that exists in itself*, or a *meaning/attribute that exists in something else*?

---

## The division (according to المَعْنى)

Looking at **what the noun means** (not its structure), it is one of two kinds:

### 1. اسْمُ الذَّات (ism adh-dhāt) — *noun of essence/entity*
Also called **اسم العَيْن** and **المَوْصُوف** (the "describable").
A noun that names a **self-subsisting thing** — something that **stands on its own** and can therefore **carry an adjective (صِفة)**.
- Test: *can you describe it?* If you can say "a ___ that is generous/big/red," it's an اسم ذات.
- **رَجُلٌ** → رَجُلٌ كَريمٌ ("a generous man") ✔
- **كِتابٌ، قَلَمٌ، ماءٌ، بَيْتٌ** — all describable entities.

These are typically **concrete / مَحْسُوس (مادّي)**: they exist in physical form, occupy space, and are perceptible — *non-abstract* nouns.

### 2. اسْمُ المَعْنى (ism al-maʿnā) — *noun of meaning/attribute*
Also called the **الصِّفة** side. A noun that names an **abstract meaning** which does **not** subsist on its own — it only exists **in something else (يقوم بغيره)**.
- **العِلْم** (knowledge), **الحِلْم** (forbearance), **الصَّبْر** (patience), **الصِّدْق** (truthfulness), **الكَرَم** (generosity), **الشَّجاعة** (courage).
- You can't point to "patience" standing in space — it exists only **in a patient person**.

---

## The link between them: المَصْدَر vs الصِّفة

The abstract meaning shows up in two linked forms:

| | The bare meaning (المَصْدَر / اسم المعنى) | The one possessing it (الصِّفة) |
|---|---|---|
| | **العِلْم** (knowledge) | **عالِمٌ** (a knower — *one who has* عِلم) |
| | **الكَرَم** (generosity) | **كَريمٌ** (generous — *has* كرم) |
| | **الشَّجاعة** (courage) | **شُجاعٌ** (brave — *has* شجاعة) |
| | **الصِّدْق** (truthfulness) | **صادِقٌ** (truthful) |

> A **صِفة** essentially means: *a person/thing **that possesses that meaning inside it***. **عالِم** = one in whom **العِلم** subsists.

---

## Map

```
الاسم (by meaning)
├── اسم ذات  (الموصوف / العين)  → a thing that exists on its own, describable
│      └── usually محسوس/مادّي (concrete): رجل، كتاب، قلم، ماء
└── اسم معنى (الصفة side)        → an attribute that exists only in something else
        ├── المصدر:  عِلم، حِلم، صبر، كرم، شجاعة   (the bare meaning)
        └── الصِّفة:  عالِم، كريم، شجاع، صادق        (the possessor of the meaning)
```

---

## Examples in context

- **رَجُلٌ عالِمٌ** — *a knowledgeable man*: **رجل** = اسم ذات (the describable entity); **عالِم** = صفة (he possesses **العِلم**, an اسم معنى).
- **العِلْمُ نُورٌ** — *Knowledge is light*: **العِلم** here is an **اسم معنى** standing as the subject.

## Why it matters
These terms — **اسم ذات، اسم معنى، موصوف، صفة، مصدر** — recur throughout naḥw (e.g. in describing الصفة/النعت, الحال, التمييز). Knowing *this is an entity* vs *this is an attribute* tells you in advance how a word can function and what can attach to it.
