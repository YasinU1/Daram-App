# النَّعْت (The Adjective / Qualifier)

> **Video:** [Al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw: النعت](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pMDPWIVBTGY) · playlist index 140
> **Book:** al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw — **starts p. 156** (heading: **النوع الأول: النَّعْت**), continues on **p. 157**.
> Located under **المقصد الثاني → المعمول بالتبعية (التوابع)** — the first of the five *followers* (توابع): نعت، بدل، عطف بيان، عطف نسق (all in this same chapter, pp. 156–159).
> **Topic:** what an adjective is, **what words can act as one**, and the two kinds — الحقيقي vs السببي — with the exact agreement rules of each.

---

## Names of the parts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **النَّعْت** | the adjective (also called **الصِّفة**) |
| **المَنْعُوت** | the described word (also called **المَوْصُوف**) |

> The teacher preferred the term **نعت** over **صِفة**: *ṣifah* is also used loosely elsewhere (in a structural sense), so **naʿt** is less ambiguous. Same thing either way — no big issue.

---

## What can be a نعت?

A naʿt must carry a **verbal / derived meaning (مشتق)** — or *resemble* the mushtaqq in doing so. Anything that behaves like a describer qualifies:

| Kind | Example (from the book) | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **مشتق** (a true derived word) | ﴿وقالَ رَجُلٌ **مُؤمِنٌ**﴾ | direct verbal meaning ("believing") |
| **اسم الإشارة** (demonstrative) | ﴿اذهَبوا بِقَميصي **هٰذا**﴾ = *بقميصي المُشار إليه* | *hādhā* means "the one **pointed to**" — a describing sense |
| **ذو** and its branches (أولو…) | ﴿جَنَّتَيْنِ **ذَواتَيْ** أُكُلٍ خَمْطٍ﴾ | *dhū* borrows the meaning of the noun after it ("possessing…") |
| **مصدر غير ميمي** (verbal noun, non-*mīmī*) | ﴿بِدَمٍ **كَذِبٍ**﴾ | a maṣdar used **for emphasis** — *كَذِب* (a lie) in place of *كاذب* = an absolute/extreme lie. ⚠️ the **maṣdar mīmī cannot** do this |
| **الاسم المنسوب** (nisbah, ends in ـيّ) | ﴿كَوكَبٌ **دُرِّيٌّ**﴾ | the ـيّ ending turns a noun into an attribute ("pearly / shining") |
| **الأسماء الموصولة** (relatives, start with hamzat al-waṣl) | ﴿اعبُدوا رَبَّكُمُ **الَّذي** خَلَقَكُم﴾ | |
| **أسماء العدد** (numbers) | ﴿في ظُلُماتٍ **ثَلاثٍ**﴾ | |

> **The nisbah tip from the video:** add ـيّ to a noun and it becomes an adjective — e.g. *ديمقراطية* (democracy) → *ديمقراطيّ* (democratic); *ثورة* (revolution) → *ثوريّ* (revolutionary); *دُرّة* (pearl) → *دُرِّيّ* (pearly/shining). Same mechanism as ﴿كوكبٌ دُرّيّ﴾.

## The two types of نعت

```
النَّعْت
├── حقيقي (real)   → describes a quality IN the منعوت itself
│        fāʿil = hidden pronoun (ضمير مستتر)
│        agrees with متبوعه in ALL 4 pairs
└── سببي (causal) → describes a quality in something CONNECTED to the منعوت
         fāʿil = a visible noun (اسم ظاهر) right after it
         agrees with متبوعه in 2 things, with the FOLLOWING noun in gender + number
```

### 1. النَّعْت الحقيقي — *real / direct*

Describes a state or quality of the **منعوت itself**. Its فاعل is a **hidden pronoun** referring back to the منعوت.

It **follows the متبوع in all four pairs (the "ten things")**:

| Follows in… | options |
|---|---|
| الإعراب | rafʿ / naṣb / jarr |
| التعريف والتنكير | definite / indefinite |
| التذكير والتأنيث | masculine / feminine |
| التوحيد والتثنية والجمع | singular / dual / plural |

- ﴿وقالَ رَجُلٌ مُؤمِنٌ﴾ — *مؤمن* describes *رجل* directly; both are indefinite, masc., sing., marfūʿ.

### 2. النَّعْت السَّبَبِي — *causal*

Describes a quality in **something linked to** the منعوت (not the منعوت itself). Its فاعل is a **visible noun that comes after it**.

- **Follows the متبوع** in only **إعراب** + **تعريف/تنكير**.
- **Matches the noun after it** (its فاعل) in **gender & number**.

﴿مِنْ هٰذِهِ القَرْيَةِ **الظّالِمِ** أهلُها﴾ — *الظالم* is majrūr + definite **like القرية**, but its gender/number track **أهلُها** (the actual doers of the wrongdoing), not القرية.

- There **must be a ضمير** in the sababi naʿt returning to the منعوت. Sometimes a **visible noun replaces** that pronoun: ﴿شَديدِ **العِقابِ**﴾.

---

## Dropping the منعوت (حذف المنعوت)

The described word can be **removed**, and the naʿt **stands in its place**:

- ﴿واعمَلوا **صالِحًا**﴾ → أي *عَمَلًا صالِحًا* — "do righteous **[deeds]**": *عملاً* (the منعوت) is dropped, *صالحاً* carries it.

---

## Why it matters

النعت is the **first of the five توابع** (نعت · بدل · عطف بيان · عطف نسق) — the words that take their iʿrāb *by following* another word rather than from a governing operator. Nailing the **حقيقي vs سببي** split is the whole game: it tells you **which word an adjective must agree with** — the one before it, or the noun after it — which is exactly where students misread ﴿الظالمِ أهلُها﴾-type structures.
