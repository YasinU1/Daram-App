# 01 — قَبْلَ الكِتاب (Before the Book)

> **Series:** Al-Kubrā fī an-Naḥw (الكُبْرى في النَّحْو)
> **Video:** [قبل الكتاب](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZC8N2PnDzQ)
> **Type:** Author's preface — orientation, not yet grammar content.

---

## What this book is

- **الكُبْرى في النَّحْو** ("The Greater [book] in Grammar") is the **advanced/comprehensive** level in a graded series. It builds on two earlier, simpler primers — **الصُّغْرى** (aṣ-Ṣughrā, "the smaller") and **الوُسْطى** (al-Wusṭā, "the middle") — which were originally separate books but are now **merged into one print** (it opens with aṣ-Ṣughrā material and closes with al-Wusṭā material), with *al-Kubrā* layered on top.
- Same **methodology, chapter order (الأبواب), headings, and structure** as the earlier primers — but with the **advanced material** an upper-level student needs added in.
- Originally written by the teacher as **his own personal notes** (مذكّرات), then opened up for other students to benefit from.

## Sources it draws on

Compiled from the major works of the grammarians (كتب النحو), e.g. commentaries such as **شرح الجامي / الفوائد الضيائية** (Mullā Jāmī) and other classical references. It is a synthesis "everyone draws from these anyway."

## The defining aim: naḥw **for the Qurʾān**

This is the key distinguishing feature of the book:

- Standard naḥw texts leave out many **مسائل** (issues) that you actually meet when reading the **Qurʾān**, or mention them only in passing.
- So whenever a Qurʾānic construction needs a grammar point that the usual texts skip, **this book adds it in** — even mid-series, the videos are "not final" and points get added when a Qurʾānic need (مسألة قرآنية) appears.
- Framing: the book is effectively a **linguistic introduction to the Qurʾān (مدخل لُغوي للقرآن)** — it prepares you to understand *how the language of the Qurʾān works*.

## Method / style choices

- **Clear text over terse text:** chooses long, explicit explanation (matn wāḍiḥ) rather than the dense, abbreviated style of classical mutūn — so the student is not left confused. (At times this makes it feel slow/repetitive — that's deliberate.)
- **Examples everywhere (الأمثلة):** prefers concrete examples — wherever possible **Qurʾānic verses (آيات)** — over abstract definitions.
- Intended as a **reference** you can return to, useful for those studying the longer commentaries and for tackling the Qurʾān.

## Key terms introduced

| Arabic | Translit. | Meaning here |
|---|---|---|
| النَّحْو | an-naḥw | Arabic grammar / syntax |
| الكُبْرى / الوُسْطى / الصُّغْرى | al-Kubrā / al-Wusṭā / aṣ-Ṣughrā | the graded levels (greater / middle / smaller) |
| البابُ (ج الأبواب) | al-bāb | a chapter / topic-section |
| المَسْأَلة (ج المَسائل) | al-masʾalah | a specific grammatical issue/question |
| المَتْن | al-matn | the base text being explained |

## Takeaway

A scene-setting preface. No grammar yet — but it fixes the **lens** for the whole series: every rule is taught with an eye to **reading and understanding the Qurʾān**, explained plainly and with abundant examples. Real content begins next, in **مقدمة الكتاب**.
